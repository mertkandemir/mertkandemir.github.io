My day job

My days are spent inside production.

At IAS I work on the CANIAS ERP Production (PRD) module — standard-product and customer-specific development. The topics I spend most of my time on:

Production orders

Creating, releasing and tracking work orders through their full lifecycle.

Reports that turn shop-floor data into decisions for planners and managers.

My journey

I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at İzmir University. My career started on the factory floor: writing shop-floor applications connected to PLCs and SCADA, and serving as second project lead on the turnkey installation of a feed mill in Sakarya.

I've been at IAS since 2022, working on the production module of CANIAS ERP — a journey that took me all the way to South Korea with the Hyundai project.
